\section{Conclusion}\label{sec:conclusion}
\cage combines hard policy admission with temporal node-risk screening,
destination-use history, and an empirical headroom check. Its trace replay
shows fewer migration decisions than a current-state learned control and
less destination concentration than least-loaded selection. The procedure
obeys its configured checks across the reported policy overlays at low
in-memory computational cost. The evidence supports this decision-level
result; establishing post-migration resource safety and application benefit
requires an executable artifact and an evaluation that includes placement
feedback.
