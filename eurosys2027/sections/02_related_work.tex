\section{Related Work}\label{sec:related}
\paragraph{Admission and placement.}
Kubernetes already separates candidate filtering from scoring and provides
reservation and pre-bind extension points \citep{kubernetes2026}.
\cage builds on this separation conceptually; its distinct question is how
temporal node risk and source retention interact under a fixed policy mask.
Pallewatta and Babar describe policy-as-code management of edge--cloud
microservices \citep{pallewatta2024policy}. Thijsman et al. study remotely
attested Kubernetes workers \citep{thijsman2024attested}. Such mechanisms
can supply policy and trust decisions. A synthetic Boolean attribute in
our replay does not substitute for their enforcement or attestation work.

\paragraph{Resource and performance management.}
NetMARKS uses service-mesh communication metrics for placement
\citep{wojciechowski2021netmarks}. KaiS studies collaborative scheduling in
Kubernetes-oriented edge--cloud networks \citep{shen2023kais}. These systems
motivate richer placement objectives than node utilization alone. Our
evaluation does not model communication costs, so it cannot establish an
advantage over them on their objectives.

FIRM addresses SLO-oriented resource management \citep{qiu2020firm}; Sage
studies performance debugging \citep{gan2021sage}; and Sinan uses data to
manage microservice resources under QoS constraints \citep{zhang2021sinan}.
Temporal information is therefore not itself a novel resource-management
technique. Here it predicts a specific node-utilization event and controls
retention or destination admission. That target is more limited than
end-to-end QoS.

\paragraph{Fairness and empirical margins.}
Madej et al. examine priority-based fair edge scheduling
\citep{madej2020fair}. We instead count destination selections and report
their spread using Jain's index \citep{jain1984fairness}. This does not imply
fairness in useful work or user service quality. Likewise, the Q95 margin
is a simple empirical persistence-residual rule, not a new uncertainty
estimator. The contribution evaluated here is the behavior of these
components together, with ablations that expose their costs and limits.
